# 01 — Branding (draft, needs your sign-off before Sprint 2)

## Direction
Technical-credibility-first, not flashy. Think "systems architect," not "creative portfolio."
Recruiters and CTOs should feel this looks like something a production engineer built —
clean, fast, legible, dark-mode-friendly (your audience is engineers who default to dark mode).

## Proposed palette
| Token          | Hex       | Use                                  |
|----------------|-----------|---------------------------------------|
| `bg-primary`   | `#0B1120` | Page background (dark slate/navy)    |
| `bg-surface`   | `#111827` | Cards, panels                        |
| `accent`       | `#22D3EE` | CTAs, links, highlight (cyan)        |
| `accent-alt`   | `#6366F1` | Secondary accent (indigo, for tags)  |
| `text-primary` | `#E5E7EB` | Body text                            |
| `text-muted`   | `#9CA3AF` | Secondary text, captions             |
| `success`      | `#34D399` | Metrics/achievements (e.g. "+70%")   |

Light-mode equivalents can be derived (invert lightness, keep accent hues) — theme switcher is
already in your Sprint 2 component list.

## Typography
- Headings: **Space Grotesk** or **Sora** (geometric, technical feel, free on Google Fonts)
- Body: **Inter**
- Code/architecture labels: **JetBrains Mono**

## Logo / mark
Simple monogram: "FM" in a rounded-square badge, or a minimal node-graph icon (nod to
agentic/graph-based systems — LangGraph, network diagrams) instead of literal initials.
Cursor can generate SVG variants once you pick a direction.

## Tone of voice (for About / Home copy)
Direct, outcome-first, quantified. Match the register already in your CV: lead with the
mechanism (what you built/architected), close with the measured result. Avoid generic
"passionate about AI" framing — your material is strong enough to skip it.
