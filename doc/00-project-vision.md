# 00 — Project Vision

## Who this is for
Farid J. Maleki — Senior Data Scientist / AI Engineer (conversational AI, agentic orchestration,
LLMOps, LLM-integrated backends). 6+ years across telecom, fintech-adjacent, compliance/finance,
and pharma. Currently: MCI R&D Center. Based in the Netherlands, open to EU relocation
(incl. Frankfurt) and worldwide remote/hybrid/on-site.

## Primary goal
Give recruiters, hiring managers, CTOs, and tech leads a fast, credible way to answer three
questions in under 60 seconds:
1. **Can this person ship production AI systems?** (not just prototypes)
2. **What exactly did they build, and what was the measurable impact?**
3. **Are they a fit for my team / role / domain?**

## Target audience (in priority order)
1. Technical recruiters / talent partners screening for AI engineering roles
2. Hiring managers / CTOs evaluating technical depth
3. Tech leads assessing architecture judgment and system design maturity
4. ADPlist mentees / peers (secondary — credibility signal, not primary CTA)

## Core value proposition
"I design and ship production-grade conversational AI and agentic systems in regulated,
customer-facing environments — not demos." Evidence: on-prem voice AI call center (70% latency
reduction), anomaly detection cutting MTTR to <1hr, RAG platforms with 95%+ retrieval accuracy,
matching systems with 33–70% relevance/completeness gains.

## Success metrics
- Resume/CV downloads
- Contact form submissions / LinkedIn click-throughs
- Time on Projects and Architecture pages (proxy for technical credibility read-through)
- Return visits from recruiter domains (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever referrers)
